Pillar 01

Personal Health

Personal health is the practice of understanding and caring for physical, mental, emotional and social wellbeing. It includes daily habits, prevention, informed decisions and knowing when to seek qualified help.

🧼

Personal hygiene

Learn daily practices that reduce the spread of germs and support comfort and dignity.

  • Handwashing with soap and clean water
  • Oral, skin, hair and body care
  • Menstrual hygiene and clean clothing
  • Safe hygiene habits at home and school
🥗

Nutrition & hydration

Understand how food and fluids support growth, energy and normal body functions.

  • Food groups and dietary variety
  • Balanced meals and portion awareness
  • Food labels and informed choices
  • Safe drinking water and hydration
🧠

Mental & emotional wellbeing

Build awareness of emotional health, coping skills and supportive relationships.

  • Recognising stress and emotions
  • Healthy coping and relaxation
  • Sleep, rest and social connection
  • When and how to seek support
🩺

Disease prevention

Explore everyday prevention and the role of appropriate healthcare.

  • Vaccination and preventive services
  • Infectious and noncommunicable diseases
  • Screening and early health checks
  • Responsible use of medicines
🤝

Relationships & life-stage health

Learn respectful, age-appropriate health information across different life stages.

  • Healthy boundaries and communication
  • Puberty and body changes
  • Reproductive health education
  • Respect, consent and personal safety
🛡️

Personal safety

Recognise everyday risks and practise safer choices.

  • Home, road and workplace safety
  • Sun and heat protection
  • Substance-use risk awareness
  • Emergency contacts and preparedness
Pillar 02

Environmental Health

Environmental health examines how the physical, chemical and biological conditions around us influence health. Clean and safe environments help prevent illness, protect ecosystems and support thriving communities.

💧

Water, sanitation & hygiene

Understand safe water access, sanitation systems and hygiene practices.

  • Safe water sources and storage
  • Household water treatment basics
  • Toilets, drainage and handwashing
  • Preventing water contamination
♻️

Waste management

Explore how responsible waste handling protects people and the environment.

  • Reduce, reuse and recycle
  • Safe household waste separation
  • Proper disposal of hazardous waste
  • Community clean-up and litter prevention
🌬️

Air quality & pollution

Learn how indoor and outdoor air quality can affect health.

  • Common sources of air pollution
  • Ventilation and indoor smoke
  • Transport and industrial emissions
  • Reducing exposure on poor-air days
🍎

Food safety

Understand how safe food handling helps prevent foodborne illness.

  • Clean hands and food-contact surfaces
  • Separate raw and ready-to-eat foods
  • Cook food thoroughly
  • Chill perishable food promptly
🦟

Housing, pests & vector control

Explore healthy housing and ways to reduce disease risks from pests and vectors.

  • Ventilation, light and damp prevention
  • Remove standing water where possible
  • Store food and waste securely
  • Use locally recommended pest controls
🌡️

Climate & community health

Understand how environmental changes can influence health and safety.

  • Heat, flooding and extreme weather
  • Climate-sensitive health risks
  • Green spaces and active transport
  • Community preparedness and resilience
Learning in action

Turn knowledge into everyday practice

Use these simple activities for personal reflection, classroom discussion or community learning. Adapt them to age, ability, local conditions and school or workplace guidance.

1. Personal health check-in

Reflect on sleep, movement, nutrition, hydration, hygiene and stress. Choose one realistic habit to strengthen this week.

2. Home environment observation

Look for safe drinking-water storage, waste separation, food-storage practices, ventilation and places where water collects. Do not handle hazardous materials.

3. Community health map

Identify local health-supporting resources such as parks, clinics, safe walking routes, clean-water points and waste collection services.

4. Teach-back activity

Explain one personal-health topic and one environmental-health topic in your own words, then share one practical action others can take.

Important learning and safety note: This page provides general educational information, not personal medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or environmental testing. For symptoms or health concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional. Follow local public-health and environmental authorities for water safety, food recalls, pollution alerts, sanitation and hazardous-waste instructions. Never touch unknown chemicals, medical waste or contaminated materials.
